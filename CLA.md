# CollabGod Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, draft. **This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** See [ADR-0012](docs/adr/0012-contributor-agreement.md) for why it exists and what is still open.

This agreement is between you and the maintainer of CollabGod. It lets the project use what you contribute, and it promises you that your work stays available as free software.

## In short

- You keep the copyright in your work.
- You give the maintainer a broad license to use it, including under license terms other than the project's current one.
- In return, any release that contains your work will always also be available under a free and open-source license.
- You confirm the work is yours to give.

The sections below are the agreement. The summary above is not.

## 1. Who and what

- **You** are the person, or the organization you act for, who submits a contribution.
- **The maintainer** is Chad Malmrose, together with his successors and assigns (section 8).
- **The project** is the CollabGod software and documentation in the repository `chadalon/collabgod` and wherever it moves to.
- **A contribution** is anything you submit to the project to be included in it: code, documentation, designs, tests, or other material, in a pull request or by any other means.

## 2. You keep your copyright

This agreement is a license, not a transfer. You remain free to use your contribution in any other way.

## 3. Copyright license

You grant the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to:

- reproduce, modify, publicly display, publicly perform and distribute your contribution and works based on it; and
- license it to others, and let them do the same, under the project's current license or under any other license terms, subject to section 4.

## 4. The maintainer's promise to you

Whenever the maintainer distributes a version of the project that contains your contribution, that version will also be made available to the public under GPL-3.0-or-later, or under another license that the Open Source Initiative has approved or that the Free Software Foundation lists as a free software license.

The maintainer may offer the same version under other terms as well. Doing so never removes the free version.

This promise binds whoever holds the maintainer's rights under this agreement.

## 5. Patent license

If you hold patent claims that your contribution infringes, alone or combined with the project as it was when you submitted it, you grant the maintainer and everyone who receives the project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license under those claims to make, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer the contribution and the project.

## 6. What you confirm

- You wrote the contribution, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement.
- If your employer or anyone else has rights in what you create, you have their permission, or they have given up those rights for this contribution.
- Where any part comes from someone else, you say so in the pull request, with its source and its license.
- Where you used software tools to produce any part, including AI assistants, you have reviewed that part and you take responsibility for it as your own contribution.
- You will tell the maintainer if you learn that any of these statements is wrong.

## 7. No obligation, no warranty

The maintainer does not have to use your contribution. Except for the statements in section 6, you provide it as it is, without warranty of any kind.

## 8. Transfer

The maintainer may transfer this agreement, and the rights under it, to a company or other organization that takes over running the project, or to a later maintainer. The promise in section 4 goes with it. You do not need to sign again.

## 9. Later versions

A new version of this agreement applies only to contributions you make after you agree to it. Contributions you made under this version stay under this version.

## 10. How to agree

In your first pull request, add one line to [`CLA-SIGNATURES.md`](CLA-SIGNATURES.md) with your name, your GitHub username, the date and the version of this agreement. Adding that line means you agree to this agreement for that contribution and for every later one, until you agree to a newer version.

If you contribute for an organization, say so on your line and name it.
