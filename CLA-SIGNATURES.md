# Contributor agreement signatures

People who have agreed to the [CollabGod Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Add your own line in your first pull request, at the end of the list. Never add or edit a line for someone else.

Format:

```
- Full Name (@github-username), YYYY-MM-DD, CLA 1.0
```

For an organization: `- Full Name (@github-username) for Organization Name, YYYY-MM-DD, CLA 1.0`
